TRAILING P/E:
Current market share price divided by actual trailing 12-month earnings per share.
FORWARD P/E:
Price divided by consensus estimated next 12-month earnings per share.
PEG RATIO:
P/E ratio divided by projected EPS growth rate (< 1.0 indicates value).
